About

I design and build backend systems and the APIs that sit behind them — authentication and authorization, data consistency under concurrent access, and recovery behavior when connections fail mid-operation. My work leans toward the parts of an application that determine correctness rather than the parts that determine appearance: session lifecycle, idempotent writes, ordering guarantees, and graceful degradation under partial failure.

I'm early in my career, but I treat every project as an exercise in systems thinking: what invariants does this component guarantee, what happens when a client disconnects at the wrong moment, and how does the design fail safely rather than silently.

Currently building: Realtime-Canvas — a collaborative editing surface addressing operation ordering, conflict resolution, and reconnect/replay semantics over WebSockets.
